UCB Swadhin Account
It is a Foreign Currency Account for the freelancer professionals. You can seamlessly receive your remittance earnings in UCB Swadhin Account in foreign currency and easily convert it into local currency (BDT) with a linked transactional CASA account.
Eligibility: Any Bangladeshi freelancer or service exporter
50% of net export earnings can be retained in UCB Swadhin Account in foreign currency and can be used for bonafide business purpose.
Single Dual Currency Debit Card (for both the accounts ERQ & BDT Savings) – 1st Year Free
Account Maintenance Fee – Free
SMS Banking Fee - Free
Internet Banking (UCB One) – Free
Account Opening Deposit – Not required
No minimum balance requirement
New customer must have to open a Savings/Current Account as link account
